Life Story

Early days
Grew up fascinated by how the brain perceives the world. Got hooked on computers young — started coding before I could explain what I was doing.
2022
Dug deep into machine learning and computer vision. Realized perception — how machines see and understand the world — was the problem I wanted to spend my life on.
2023
Started working on neuromorphic vision and depth estimation. First serious research project. Learned that good research is mostly relentless iteration.

Now
