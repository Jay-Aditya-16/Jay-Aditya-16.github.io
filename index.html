Syntheta AI
Founder & CEO · Active

Foundation model for physical AI training data. Pre-trained on the world's factory and robotics datasets — generates unlimited, customer-specific, real-indistinguishable training data on demand. Leading world model development.

What it does

Expands real factory, robot, and drone footage into labeled training data for edge cases: low light, motion, occlusion, new viewpoints — without re-capturing.

My role

Founder & CEO. Leading world model development — the core generative pipeline that keeps every synthetic frame anchored to the original scene geometry and labels.

How it works

Bring a real capture → define conditions (lighting, motion, occlusion, sensor variation) → generate validated dataset with labels and provenance carried forward.

Origin

Built from the ruins of Sentrix Labs (shut down). The data bottleneck for physical AI was the real problem — Syntheta is the answer.
